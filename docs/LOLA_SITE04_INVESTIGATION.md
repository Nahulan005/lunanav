\# NASA LOLA Site04 Investigation



\## Data source

NASA GSFC Planetary Geosciences Data Archive

Product: LOLA data for candidate South Pole landing sites

Candidate: Site04 - Shackleton Rim



\## Downloaded datasets

\- Site04\_final\_adj.xyzi

\- Site04\_ldec.tif

\- Site04\_surf.tif

\- Site04\_toterr.tif



\## Point-cloud inspection

\- Total records: 983,913

\- Record format: 4 little-endian float64 values

\- Fields: X, Y, Z, RDRid

\- X/Y/Z coordinates are documented in kilometres.



\## Raster inspection

\- Dimensions: 3200 x 3200

\- Pixel spacing: 5 metres

\- LDEC positive pixels: 937,381

\- Total pixels: 10,240,000

\- Observation coverage: approximately 9.15%



\## Important limitations

\- Pixel registration verification is pending.

\- XYZI Z reference and datum interpretation require confirmation.

\- Surface raster includes interpolated values in empty pixels.

\- LDEC observation counts must be considered when interpreting coverage.

\- No landing suitability or safety classification has been produced.



\## Current status

This is preliminary data investigation only.

No production terrain integration has been completed.
